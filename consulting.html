Consulting

Central Bering Sea Fishermen’s Association (CBSFA) · 2021–2023

Worked with a legal team and association members to develop cohesive, science-based economic analysis and provide live testimony leading to a key ruling selecting the CBSFA-favored management alternative.

Property and Environment Research Center (PERC) · 2020–2023

National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA) · 2014–2016

Worked with NOAA economists to develop price projections for the SAFE economic status reports of groundfish fisheries off Alaska.
